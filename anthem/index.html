Divine Anthem

One button, a whole track with band, beat and choir. The machine chooses everything, sketches several songs in silence and gives you only the best.

  1. Drag the MIDI into Studio One; Beat, Bass, Chords, Pad, Melody, Answer and Choir each land on their own track, always in that order.
  2. Put your drums on Beat, your instruments on the others, and Olympus Micro on the Choir track.
  3. Press KEYS in Olympus and pick the vowels you like.
  4. Press play.
