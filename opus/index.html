Divine Opus

One button, a whole symphony with a choir. The machine chooses everything, sketches several versions in silence and gives you only the best.

  1. Drag the MIDI into Studio One; every instrument lands on its own track.
  2. Put BBC Symphony Orchestra Discover on the orchestra tracks, and Olympus Micro on the Choir track.
  3. Press KEYS in Olympus and pick the vowels you like.
  4. Press play.
